About

A bar with a pool table in the middle.

Jungle Bar is in Paramaribo. You come in for a drink, someone racks the balls, and a while later it's past midnight.

Enjoyment is priority

That's our motto, and it's how we run the place. Nobody is in a hurry, nobody gets a hard time, and the point is to have a good evening.

We keep the bar simple: beer, rum, whisky, mixers and soft drinks. There's a pool table, music, and seats inside and out.

We're open from 15:00 to 02:00, seven days a week.
